BIO


VIET LINH (She/Her) is a multi-hyphenated theatre maker, with experience in directing, playwriting, scenic, and lighting design. As a proud Vietnamese artist who is living her childhood dream in NYC, Viet Linh is dedicated to creating original, radically inclusive works that uplift marginalized identities. She is currently pursuing a BFA in Drama at NYU Tisch's Playwrights Horizons Theatre School (PHTS), with a minor in Integrated Design & Media at NYU Tandon.


UPCOMING SHOWS: Mojada (March 28-29), The Witch Cat Cafe (April 9-11), Mỳ Tôm (April 16-18)
